# Privacy Policy — Mahjong Score

Last updated: 2026-09-19

Mahjong Score (iPhone) does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network
access of any kind in the iPhone app.

## What the app stores

The only data the app keeps is the table settings you enter — round wind,
seat wind, dealer flag, and base tai — saved with Apple's standard
`UserDefaults` so they're remembered the next time you open the app. This
data:

- never leaves your device
- is not visible to the developer
- is not shared with any third party
- can be erased at any time by deleting the app

## Photo recognition (Mac app only)

The Mac companion app has an optional photo-recognition mode that sends a
photo of a mahjong hand to Anthropic's Claude API to identify the tiles.
That feature does not exist in the iPhone app — the iPhone app has no
camera or photo-library access, and no network permission. If a future
version of the iPhone app adds this feature, this policy will be updated
first and the feature will be clearly optional.
